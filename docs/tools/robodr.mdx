## Check RoboDR action policy

Action ID: `tools.robodr.check_action_policy`

Check a robot action against the SOC-side rules before it is prepared: catalog action, how many sign-offs it needs (single or two-person), and morphology (safe_pause, request_safe_state and rollback_config only for wheeled_mobile, arm, aerial or other; never rollback_config on a quadruped). ## Request RoboDR robot action

Action ID: `tools.robodr.request_action`

Send one signed-off catalog action request (robot_restrict_motion, robot_quarantine_node, robot_isolate_network, robot_record_evidence, robot_safe_pause, robot_request_safe_state, robot_rollback_config) to a robot's control plane over mutual TLS. Refuses unless platform form submissions prove the requester and one approver (two for the two-person actions), all different platform users, signed off this exact request; a two-person action names its two approvers to the robot (approved_by), which the robot's executor requires. The robot judges it against its safety state and may refuse it. recorded_not_executed, refused and failed are reported as not applied; pending is not yet in force; applied (request_safe_state, rollback_config through a platform adapter) is final and not reversible; in_force is the robot's own word (verified or applied, and not held after a reboot). A robot_quarantine_node in force reports containment: full (contained), partial, guid_only (GUID-prefix drop only, not IP-contained) or unknown; only full is contained. Recorded by the platform (the run, requester and approvers) before it is sent; refused if the configured audit sink does not confirm it. audit_log says written (sink confirmed) or no_sink (no audit sink configured: platform log only).

Ask a robot to revert a signed-off action request. Needs the same platform sign-off as a request (two approvers to revert a two-person action such as robot_safe_pause; their names are sent as approved_by). Reverting a safe_pause releases the pause and never resumes a mission. request_safe_state, rollback_config and record_evidence are not reversible (refused, not_reversible); a request that never took effect answers not_applied; an executed one answers pending, then reverted. Recorded by the platform before it is sent; refused if the configured audit sink does not confirm it. audit_log says written (sink confirmed) or no_sink (no audit sink configured: platform log only).

## Verify RoboDR finding signature

Action ID: `tools.robodr.verify_finding_signature`

Verify the detached Ed25519 per-finding signature (robodr-finding-sig) over the raw record value. Returns valid, reason and the signing key id. Verification is strict and never re-serialises the value.

Reference: [https://docs.singularityrd.com/integrations/robodr-tools](https://docs.singularityrd.com/integrations/robodr-tools)
